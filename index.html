The stone

Every slab is different. These four materials cover most of what we cut.

Granite

Hard-wearing and heat resistant. The usual choice for a busy kitchen.

Marble

Veined and soft-toned. Suits vanities, feature walls and stairs.

Quartz

Engineered and non-porous, with consistent colour from slab to slab.

Sintered stone

Large-format slabs that hold their colour outdoors.
